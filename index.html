01At Nokia

He joined Nokia in Bengaluru in June 2016. The work moved from Wi-Fi into the CBRS Domain Proxy, and from there into research simulators.

2016–2020

Development architect, then Wi-Fi development, then CBRS. The last months of this stretch were on the CBRS Domain Proxy, including a security upgrade of software components.

2020–2022

Technical lead, then technical specialist, for the CBRS Domain Proxy platform. Configuration management was generated from templates. He also worked on the Airphone 5G simulator.

2022–

Simulators for 5G-Advanced and 6G mobile research, and a Kubernetes-based Linux environment for the department’s compute.
